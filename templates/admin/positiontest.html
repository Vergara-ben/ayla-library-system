Which way is north on this plan

compass: waiting

The patron map moves the marker as people walk, using the phone’s compass to decide which way a step went. A compass gives bearings from magnetic north, but this floor plan is drawn in whatever orientation it was drawn in — so the two have to be reconciled once, here. Until this is measured the marker will walk off at a fixed angle to wherever the patron actually went, which looks far worse than not moving it at all.

Easiest way to measure it: stand at one beacon and face another — they are already on the map, so their true bearing is known. Pick the two below, point the top of the phone straight at the second beacon, and press Capture.
