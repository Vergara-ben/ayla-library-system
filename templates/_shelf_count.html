Scanning a book on this board ticks it. Scanning one that belongs elsewhere says where it should go — which is the thing tapping down a list can never tell you.

Tap a row to mark it here. Tap the cross to mark it not found, and the tag when the book has no label on it. A book confirmed here without that mark is recorded as labelled. Rows you never touch are covered by “the rest are here”, and the count records how many you confirmed one by one against how many were swept, so the shortcut shows in the history rather than hiding in it.
