AYLA beacon probe

Open this over localhost or 127.0.0.1 (never file://) in Chrome, with chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features enabled. It lists every Bluetooth advertisement nearby — recorded in your floor plan or not — and decodes the ones that are actually beacons, so you can copy their real identifiers straight into the beacon form.

Idle — press Start scanning.
