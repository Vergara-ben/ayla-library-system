Calibration & mounting — optional, sensible defaults applied

Measure RSSI one metre from this beacon. The exponent defaults to 2.5, which suits a room with shelving; 2.0 is free space and 3.5 is a heavily obstructed aisle. Too low reads distances long, too high reads them short.

Height is how far above the floor the beacon is fixed. Ranging measures the straight line through the air, so without it a beacon overhead reads as further away than it is — worst directly beneath it. Leave blank if you have not measured it.
