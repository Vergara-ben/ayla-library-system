1. About these terms

These terms apply to your Ayla Public Library account and to the library's services: borrowing, the online catalogue, the indoor map and messages with library staff. By ticking “I agree” when you register, you accept them.

2. Your account

  • Give your real name and correct contact details, and keep them up to date.
  • Upload a valid ID. Library staff check it against your details before your account is activated, and may reject an application they cannot verify.
  • One account per person. Your account and library card are personal; do not lend them or share your password. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Tell the library straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

3. Borrowing

  • You may borrow up to {{ rule.max_books_per_patron }} book{{ rule.max_books_per_patron|pluralize }} at a time, for {{ rule.loan_period_days }} day{{ rule.loan_period_days|pluralize }}.
  • Return books on or before the due date. You may ask for a due-date extension; the library decides whether to grant it.
  • You cannot borrow while you have an overdue book or an unsettled lost book.
  • Handle books with care. Do not write in, mark, tear or remove pages from them.

5. Visiting the library

  • Your entry and exit are recorded when you scan in at the library's desk.
  • Follow the instructions of library staff, keep noise down, and stay out of staff-only areas.

6. The indoor map

  • The map uses Bluetooth beacons to show roughly where you are. Your position is worked out on your own phone and is used only to draw directions; it is not saved.
  • Positions and routes are approximate. Watch where you are walking and follow the library's signs and staff.

7. Suspension

The library may suspend or deactivate an account that breaks these terms, for example for repeated overdue books, a lost book that is not settled, damage, or misuse of the account. You may ask for a suspended account to be reactivated.

8. Your personal information

The library handles your information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

  • What is kept: your name, email address, contact number, address, patron type and school, the ID you upload, your borrowing history, your visits, messages you send to staff, and a record of catalogue searches and books you look up on the map.
  • Why: to verify who you are, run your account and loans, send you account and due-date emails, keep the library secure, and produce library statistics that do not name you.
  • Who sees it: authorised library staff only. It is stored with the library's hosting and email providers, and is not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Your rights: you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the library. Some records, such as unreturned loans, may have to be kept until they are settled.

9. Emails

The library emails you about your account: verification codes, approval, due dates and overdue books. These are part of the service and are not marketing.

10. Changes

The library may update these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed. Using your account after a change means you accept the new terms.

11. Contact

For questions about these terms or your information, ask at the library's front desk or send a message to library staff from your account.
