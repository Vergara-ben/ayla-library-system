AYLA beacon probe

Serve this over localhost (not file://) in Chrome with chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features enabled, then click Scan. It lists every BLE advertisement it sees and — importantly — which identifier each beacon actually broadcasts: a service UUID, a device name, or iBeacon manufacturer data. That determines how the app must match beacons to map positions.
