A short walkthrough: finding a book, following the map to its shelf, and borrowing it at the desk.

  1. Start at Home

    Your dashboard shows the books you have out, anything overdue, and whether the library is open right now. The bell at the top shows announcements and reminders.

  2. Find a book

    Tap Search at the bottom. Type a title, author, ISBN or subject, and narrow the list by subject, availability or location. Available means it is on the shelf.

  3. Open the book

    Tap a book to see its details and exactly where it is kept: room, shelf and level.

  4. Walk to the shelf

    Tap Navigate on Map. The floor plan highlights the shelf. Inside the library, Enable Navigation shows where you are as you walk (Chrome on Android; the live position setup explains how to turn it on).

  5. Sign in and borrow at the desk

    When you arrive, scan your card at the front-desk screen to sign in, and again when you leave. To borrow, bring the book to the desk and show your QR code (Account → My Library Card). Staff scan it and the book appears under Active Borrows.

  6. Return on time

    Your due dates are on Home and in Account. Need more time? Use Request extension in Account. Late books are charged a daily fine; the rules are on the back of your library card.

  7. Ask a librarian

    Have a question? Go to Account → Ask a Librarian and send a message. You will get an email when someone replies.

  8. Manage your account

    In Account you can update your details, change your password, print your library card, and see your borrowing history.
